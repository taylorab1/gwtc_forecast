# Roadmap

1. **Independent student contributions:** Run the local synthetic reference; complete one event-reweighting and one selection-normalization calculation with independent checks. Review each student after two weeks.
2. **Small real-data interfaces:** Validate one event, one selection normalization and the matched density. Extend only validated conventions to the inherited pilot and the agreed through-O3 training subset.
3. **Training and numerical qualification:** Freeze training-only scientific choices, fit the matched pair, assess inference and score precision, and exercise saved outputs through the five-event report.
4. **Discrimination and adequacy:** Qualify the observation model; complete conditional power, repeated-training checks and a bounded shared-model stress test. Apply unchanged numerical investment targets with uncertainty-aware decisions.
5. **Conditional full retrospective study:** Execute the agreed O4a/O4b score and interpretation only after valid qualification and a resource commitment. Release useful minimal reproducibility products.
6. **Possible later extension:** A genuinely prospective held-out catalog or a distinct general detectability result requires its own scientific decision. Do not start a parallel model tournament now.

Dates, per-student evidence and advisor responsibilities are in `STUDENT_MEETING_MILESTONES.md`. Later dates are review/decision points, not promises that difficult inference will finish or yield a publishable result.
