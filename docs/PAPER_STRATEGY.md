# Paper strategy and defensible endpoints

## Main paper: which mass features earn predictive value?

Working question: does the extra high-mass component learned through O3 improve prediction of O4a/O4b relative to the same bending continuum and nuisance structure without it? This differs from simply checking whether O4 lies inside an earlier model's predictive bands. The published GWTC-4 analysis already contains the latter experiment; the GWTC-5 population paper already discusses reduced high-peak support. See `LITERATURE.md`.

Aim for one central result and four evidence products: the training-only population predictions; joint and conditional-run predictive scores with numerical uncertainty; a transparent decomposition showing whether a small number of events dominates; and measurement-aware power/adequacy calibration supporting the interpretation. Release a compact reusable scoring reference and qualified input conventions. Public reusable evidence can increase usefulness, but is not a citation guarantee.

A positive delta supports incremental prediction by this component, not a particular formation mechanism. A negative delta can support predictive overcomplexity or a mismatch of this component, conditional on the qualified alternative. A near-zero delta with low power supports only a sensitivity limitation. A near-zero or unfavorable result with high qualified recovery power may support a stronger, still model-specific limitation. Do not combine these outcomes into a predetermined story that an earlier claim was wrong.

## Possible second paper (conditional, not another immediate student project)

A general detectability boundary could ask how feature amplitude/width, measurement degeneracy, selection and catalog size control external model discrimination. The supplied synthetic engine is a prototype for that question. A publishable general result would require realistic or explicitly delimited observation models, out-of-family tests and a literature comparison beyond the current targeted search. Merely plotting the toy's power curves is not a second paper.

Do not start a parallel spin/formation-channel tournament now. It adds nuisance degeneracies and model-selection flexibility before the mass comparison is understood. A physically motivated mass-spin extension becomes a separate decision only after the mass result and adequacy limitations justify it.

## Prospective extension

Retrospective O4a/O4b analysis can stand on its own if distinctive and well qualified. A prospective O4c/future extension is attractive only when the team can document genuine non-exposure, freeze choices before relevant event information is accessed, and complete it without holding the thesis hostage to an observing schedule. A future catalog label and observing run are different concepts.

## Stop/narrow branches

Stop the current claim when the measurement-aware comparison cannot distinguish the models at the frozen investment criteria. Narrow the claim when shared-model inadequacy dominates the feature interpretation. Repair and rerun affected work when an identifiable numerical defect invalidates it; this is not a scientific no-go. A validated feasibility result and reusable implementation are real student contributions even when they do not justify a standalone high-impact paper.

The advisor owns consequential choices. Students should not be pressured to find a significant peak, eliminate one, pass a power gate, or produce a publication by a grading deadline.
